प्रश्नहरू

Questions families ask

Can we mix events that aren't in a package?

Yes. Pick any events from the single-event list. Three or more events get a bundle discount close to the nearest package.

What if our ceremony runs late?

We never leave mid-ritual. Overtime is NPR 4,000 per hour, agreed on the day with the family.

Do you cover Newari, Brahmin, Chhetri, Madhesi and Tamang weddings?

Yes. We study the rituals with you before the day so we're in the right place at the right moment.

How many photos will we get?

Usually 600–1,200 edited photos per wedding day, and 250+ in the printed album.

Can relatives abroad watch live?

Yes, with the live-stream add-on (YouTube or Facebook, private link).
