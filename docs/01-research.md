# 01 — Competitor & Inspiration Research

Research date: 3 November 2026. Every site below was opened or searched for this study. Notes describe structure and ideas only. Copy is paraphrased, not copied.

**Method.** Each site was read for navigation, homepage section order, portfolio organisation, pricing display, inquiry flow, trust signals, SEO approach and tone. Fonts and colours could not be measured from fetched text. Visual notes come from known brand style and screenshots, so treat them as approximate.

**Issues to fix before launch**
1. Three handles are in use (`vibeshotsnepal`, `vibeshots_nepal`, `vibeshots.nepal`). Pick one naming rule and use it on every flyer, site and card.
2. The commercial bio says Kathmandu and the wedding bio says Bhaktapur. Use "Bhaktapur · serving all of Nepal" everywhere.
3. No website, so no Google search traffic. Competitors rank on dozens of keyword pages (see §2).
4. Prices like 39,999 and 44,999 read as discount-shop pricing. Premium studios use round numbers.

## 2. Nepal competitors

### 2.1 Wedding Story Nepal — [weddingstory.com.np](https://weddingstory.com.np/wedding-photography-kathmandu)
- **Nav:** Home, Portfolio, About, Contact, Live Weddings. Phone and "Book Now" in the header.
- **Homepage order:** hero with "packages from" price → intro → Valley venues (Yak & Yeti, Dwarika's, Patan, Bhaktapur) → package table → Google reviews → Nepali-language price block → FAQ → CTA.
- **Packages:** Bronze NPR 75,000, Silver 110,000 (marked "Most Popular"), Gold 160,000, Platinum 300,000. Extra hour NPR 5,000.
- **Terms published:** no travel charge inside the Valley, 30% advance locks the date, preview in 48 h, gallery in 2–4 weeks, film in 4–6 weeks.
- **SEO:** strongest in Nepal. About 25 city pages, venue guides, pricing guide, blog on wedding-season dates.
- **Trust:** "12+ years", "850+ weddings", 5.0 from 62 Google reviews.
- **Weak spots:** outside-Valley price not stated. Package page shows no real photos. Studio is split between Kathmandu and Butwal.
- **Borrow:** "Most Popular" badge on the middle tier, published delivery timelines, a short Nepali-language price summary, venue pages.

### 2.2 Wedding Kathmandu — [weddingkathmandu.com](https://www.weddingkathmandu.com/best-wedding-photography-packages)
- Related shop site: [photostudiokathmandu.com](https://photostudiokathmandu.com/package/wedding-photography) (same company).
- **Nav:** a mega-menu with about 40 price pages, about 20 service pages, a shop, gallery and "Achievement".
- **Pricing:** priced per day count (1-day 45–50k up to 4-day 150–170k), plus "Best", "Professional" and "Diamond" lines up to about NPR 220,000.
- **SEO:** very aggressive. Ceremony pages (Bratabandha, Gunyo Cholo, Newari, Haldi) and graduation pages.
- **Contact:** WhatsApp, Viber and phone in the top bar, plus a floating WhatsApp button.
- **Weak spots:** cluttered navigation, confusing tier names, two prices per tier, a shop-template feel.
- **Borrow:** ceremony-specific landing pages (Newari wedding, Bratabandha, Gunyo Cholo, Haldi) for SEO. Avoid the clutter.

### 2.3 Poudel Digital — [poudeldigital.com](https://poudeldigital.com/best-wedding-photographer-in-nepal)
- Butwal studio. Three tiers, each with its own WhatsApp button: Standard 65,000+, Premium Cinematic 4K 85,000+, Luxury 4K 120,000+. Extra day priced per tier.
- **Weak spots:** "Top 10 photographers" listicle that ranks itself, conflicting numbers in the FAQ, generic template.
- **Borrow:** **a WhatsApp button on every package card**, which pre-fills "I'm interested in the ___ package".

### 2.4 Directories that send Nepali leads
- [Wezoree — Kathmandu photographers](https://wezoree.com/vendors/kathmandu/photographers/filter/photography-style-editorial/apply/)
- [MyWed — Kathmandu](https://mywed.com/en/Nepal/Kathmandu-wedding-photographers)

**Action:** list Vibeshots on Wezoree, MyWed and Google Business Profile in the launch week (see `04-build-roadmap.md`).

### 2.5 Nepal commercial / production references
- [Kathmandu Films](https://www.kathmandufilms.com/) is a fixer and line producer for TVCs and international shoots, including the MotorTrend *Throttle Out* series and [Red Bull Media House](https://kathmandufilms.com/project/red-bull-media-house/). Its project pages list the client, the format and the crew role.
- [Nepal Drives](https://nepaldrives.com/nepal-drives-best-photos-of-2018) is an automotive community and a potential partner or client for motorsport work.
- [Gautam Dhimal on Behance](https://www.behance.net/gautamdhimal) is a Kathmandu commercial photographer and producer whose client list is presented as brand names.

**Gap in Nepal:** no Valley studio presents weddings *and* motorsport/brand work as two clean, premium worlds. That is Vibeshots' positioning.

## 3. India competitors

### 3.1 House On The Clouds — [houseontheclouds.com](https://www.houseontheclouds.com/)
- Squarespace. **Nav:** Photography, Films, Editorial, Contact, More (Careers, Travel). "Get In Touch" button.
- **Homepage order:** full-bleed still with no headline → short intro → award badge → award-winning films → "selected weddings" grid of couple-named stories → premium sub-brand block (Ibtida) → footer.
- **Trust:** WeddingSutra Filmmaker of the Year four years running, films listed on IMDB, 25 countries.
- **Borrow:**
  - Couple-named stories ("Name & Name, City") as the portfolio unit.
  - A **premium sub-line** for a few selected weddings. For us: a *Vibeshots Heritage* fine-art tier.
  - An awards badge row right after the intro.

### 3.2 The Wedding Story (Mumbai) — [theweddingstory.com](https://www.theweddingstory.com/)
- Squarespace. **Nav:** Stories, Photobooks, Images, Films, Pre-weddings, Music, FAQ, Contact.
- **Borrow:** photobooks and pre-weddings as **their own nav items**. This sells albums (high margin) and pre-wedding shoots (25k each for us) as products.

### 3.3 Stories by Joseph Radhik — [storiesbyjosephradhik.com](https://storiesbyjosephradhik.com/)
- A very minimal splash page with one tagline about everyone being a story. More than 450 weddings in 35 countries per [Samaro's list](https://samaro.ai/blogs/top-wedding-photographers-in-india-2025).
- **Borrow:** one strong brand line. For Vibeshots: *"Every vibe, every vow."*

- [The Wedding Salad — profile](https://www.weddingsutra.com/vendor-profile/theweddingsalad/6) (site did not load).
- [WeddingNama — reviews](https://www.wedmegood.com/profile/WeddingNama-5750/reviews) (site blocked automated fetch).
- [House on the Clouds interview — viral weddings](https://www.jgnt.co/house-on-the-clouds-indian-wedding-photography)

## 4. International references

### 4.1 Two Mann Studios (Canada) — [twomann.com](https://twomann.com/)
- WordPress. **Nav:** About, Portfolio, Reviews, Blog, Client Login, FAQ, Contact.
- **Homepage order:** hero with "See our work" → manifesto → three numbered principles → latest stories → **stats block** (weddings, awards, 5-star reviews) → testimonials → "we travel to you" location tiles → "Let's chat" CTA.
- **Story page:** title, date, short intro, **vendor credits with links** (venue, makeup, decor), full-width photo run, previous/next.
- **Contact:** a friendly "coffee or video call" framing. Fields: name, email, date, venue, message, how you heard about us. FAQ on the same page.
- **Borrow:**
  - Stats block.
  - Vendor credits on each story. Venues and makeup artists share the post, which brings free referrals and SEO.
  - FAQ next to the form.

### 4.2 Sanshine Photography (London, luxury South Asian weddings) — [sanshinephotography.com](https://www.sanshinephotography.com/)
- **Nav:** Portfolio (Weddings, Proposals, Events, Engagements, Press, Journal), Info, FAQ, Contact.
- **Homepage order:** slideshow hero → "top photographer" headline → award badges → featured galleries with a testimonial each → bio → contact form → press logo wall.
- **Inquiry form:** names, email, phone, **type of shoot**, date, venue, **budget**, how you found us, message.
- **Borrow:** budget and shoot-type fields to qualify leads. The *type* field also routes commercial inquiries.

### 4.3 Jonas Peterson (Australia) — [jonaspeterson.com](https://www.jonaspeterson.com/)
- **Nav:** Print Shop, Info, About, Packages, Portfolio, Contact.
- **Borrow:** a personal "I'm ___" founder story section, and an honest contact line that tells people they can take their time. Low-pressure tone builds trust.

### 4.4 Amy Shore (UK, automotive & motorsport) — [amyshorephotography.com](https://amyshorephotography.com/)
- Squarespace. **Nav:** Work, About, Print Shop, Car Photographer guide, Contact.
- **Homepage** is a grid of project tiles named by event or client (Goodwood Revival, Monaco Historique, Ferrari P4, Land Rover).
- **Case study:** big title → one line that names the client and partners (linked) → a short first-person brief → a long run of 40–130 images → previous/next.
- **Borrow:** this **case-study header** for every commercial project, for example "Dirt Temple × Vibeshots, shot with ___".

### 4.5 Larry Chen (USA, motorsport) — [larrychenphoto.com](https://www.larrychenphoto.com/)
- SmugMug site, content JS-rendered. Older snapshots show Commercial, Contact and Signed Prints sections. He is the official photographer for Formula Drift and Pikes Peak ([Canon profile](https://www.usa.canon.com/learning/inspiration/explorers-of-light/larry-chen)).
- **Borrow:** "Official photographer of ___" as the strongest motorsport trust line. Aim for an official role with a Nepali rally, motocross or Dirt Temple series.

### 4.6 Easton Chang (Australia, car advertising) — [Production Paradise profile](https://www.productionparadise.com/spotlight/cars-transportation-and-landscape-photography-and-motion-1047/easton-chang-31439)

## 6. Gaps no one fills (our edge)

1. **Outside-Valley pricing published**, with zones and percentages.
2. **Bhaktapur-first identity.** Durbar Square, Newari culture and local venues lead the story, where others treat Bhaktapur as one city on a list.
3. **Bikram Sambat date checker.** Clients type their saait in BS, not AD, so we accept both.
4. **Two worlds, one brand.** Wedding (warm ivory and sindoor) and Commercial (black and orange) are clearly separated, so neither dilutes the other.
5. **Real full galleries** in each story, where competitors show 6–12 photos.
6. **Transparent early-bird offer**, with a fixed percentage and a fixed deadline.
