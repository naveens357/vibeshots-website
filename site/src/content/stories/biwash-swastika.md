Swastika got ready by a tall window, and the soft light did half our work: red silk, gold and a veil that glowed every time she turned. Biwash waited downstairs in a white daura suruwal and a red topi, and their first look on the staircase was the moment the whole family had been waiting for.

Later the colours changed for the reception, a deep red lehenga and a navy suit, but the two of them stayed exactly the same: easy, laughing and completely themselves.
