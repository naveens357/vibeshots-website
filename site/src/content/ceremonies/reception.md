The reception is when everyone finally relaxes. We plan it with you and the event manager in advance: the entrance, the cake, the speeches and when the dance floor opens. We also check the stage lighting and bring our own lights where needed, so faces still look natural.

We photograph every family that comes up to the stage, then cover the food, the décor and the dancing. Before the crowd arrives, or between stage photos, we take the two of you aside for 15 to 20 minutes of couple portraits outside or in the best light we can find. Ask us early and we can also plan a short night portrait with lights.

## What we capture
- Your entrance and first moments on stage
- Stage photos with every family and group
- Speeches, cake cutting and the first dance
- The dance floor, the food and the décor
- Couple portraits during the evening
- A highlight film of the night (in film packages)
