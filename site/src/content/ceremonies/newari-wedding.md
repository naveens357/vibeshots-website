We are based in Bhaktapur and have grown up around Newari weddings, so we know how many rituals happen over the days and how quickly they move. Before the wedding, we sit down with the family to go through the order of rituals, the timings and the people who lead them.

From the gwe (betel nut) invitations and the sagan to the bride's arrival and the lasakusa welcome at her new home, we photograph each ritual in order and stay close to the elders who lead them. Newari weddings are big family events, so we make time for proper group photos with every side of the family. We also find a quiet window for the couple's portraits in their traditional dress, often in the courtyards and lanes around the house.

## What we capture
- Every ritual in order, with the elders who lead them
- The bride's departure and her welcome at the groom's home
- Traditional dress, jewellery and ritual items
- Group photos with the whole extended family
- Couple portraits around the old courtyards and lanes
- Film coverage of the rituals (in film packages)
