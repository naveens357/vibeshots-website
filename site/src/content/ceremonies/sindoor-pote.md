Sindoor and pote mark the moment you become husband and wife, and it happens fast. We position ourselves with the priest's permission before it starts, so we can see the groom's hand, the bride's face and the family behind them in the same frame.

We follow the sindoor, the pote, the rounds around the fire and the blessings that follow, then cover the family coming forward to congratulate you. Weddings run late, so we keep an eye on time and agree with you beforehand on a short window for couple portraits right after the rituals, while the outfits and the red still look their best.

## What we capture
- The sindoor being applied, clear and close
- The pote and the other final rituals
- Family and friends blessing the couple
- Your first portraits as a married couple
- Details of the sindoor, pote and jewellery
- Film coverage of the rituals (in film packages)
