Bidaai is the goodbye, and it is hard for everyone. We keep our distance, keep our voices down and let the family have the moment. Most of what we shoot here is from the side, with longer lenses, so nobody feels a camera in their face.

We photograph the last blessings at the door, the hugs with parents and siblings, the walk to the car and the final wave. We also talk to the family beforehand about any traditions at their home, so we know where to be and what not to miss.

## What we capture
- The last blessings and rituals at home
- Hugs and goodbyes with parents, siblings and friends
- The walk to the car and the departure
- Quiet, honest family reactions
- Film coverage of the farewell (in film packages)
