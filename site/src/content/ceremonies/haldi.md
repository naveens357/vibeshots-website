Haldi is fast and messy, and the best photos come from being ready before the turmeric flies. We check the plan with the family the day before: whose house, what time, who applies the haldi first. On the day we cover the whole ritual, from the paste being mixed to the last cousin getting a yellow face.

We keep our gear covered and stay close without getting in anyone's way. Elders go first, so we make sure each of them is in at least one clear photo with the bride or groom. Once it calms down, we take a few minutes for portraits while the colour is still fresh, then step back so everyone can clean up.

## What we capture
- The haldi paste and the setting being prepared
- Each family member putting haldi on, elders first
- The laughter, the chasing and the yellow faces
- Close-ups of hands, skin and flowers
- Short portraits of the bride or groom in their haldi look
- A short highlight film (in film packages)
