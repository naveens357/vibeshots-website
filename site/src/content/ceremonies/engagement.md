An engagement is often the first time both families stand together, and it is also where you will get your first proper photos as a couple. We arrive early to photograph the venue, the rings and the trays of fruit, sweets and gifts before guests sit down.

During the ceremony we follow the tika, the ring exchange and the blessings closely, and we time our shots around the priest and the family. Afterwards we speak with both sides to arrange the group photos quickly: immediate family, then extended family, then friends, so nobody waits too long. Before the meal starts, we take the two of you aside for 15 to 20 minutes of couple portraits.

## What we capture
- Rings, tika thali, gifts and venue details
- The ring exchange and blessings from both families
- Organised group photos of both families
- Relaxed couple portraits
- Candid moments between guests
- A short highlight film (in film packages)
