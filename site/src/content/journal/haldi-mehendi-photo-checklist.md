Haldi and mehendi are the loudest, happiest days of a Nepali wedding. A little planning means none of the best moments get missed.

## The checklist
1. The bride's hands with fresh mehendi, before and after
2. The mehendi artist at work
3. Grandparents applying haldi first
4. Cousins mid-laugh
5. The haldi splash (protect your phone!)
6. Mother and daughter together
7. Song and dance circles
8. The décor before guests arrive
9. A group photo while everyone is still clean
10. Quiet portraits after the chaos

## Light matters
Open courtyards and windows work best. Ask your decorator to leave one side open to daylight.
