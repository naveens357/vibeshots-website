Families often ask us for "a price" before anything else. That is fair. But a Nepali wedding is rarely one day, so the honest answer depends on how many events you want covered, for how long, and by how many people. Below we share our own numbers so you can see how it adds up. All prices are current Vibeshots prices in NPR and can change, so please confirm before you book.

## What decides the cost of wedding photography in Nepal?

Five things move the price more than anything else:

- **Number of events.** Haldi, mehendi, engagement, the wedding day and the reception are each a separate job for us.
- **Hours on the day.** A groom side wedding with janti usually runs longer than a bride side wedding.
- **Team size.** One photographer, or extra photographers and videographers so no moment is missed.
- **Film and extras.** Cinematic highlight films, a drone where it is permitted, a same-day edit reel.
- **Prints and albums.** A canvas for the wall or a lay-flat album for the family.

Travel matters too, which we cover further down.

## Are wedding packages cheaper than booking each event?

Yes. Our packages bundle several events, and the bundle costs less than adding the same events up one by one. Each package is priced for the bride side, the groom side, or both families together.

- **Saath:** 70,000 bride side, 75,000 groom side, 1,07,000 both families.
- **Mangal:** 1,18,000 bride side, 1,23,000 groom side, 1,55,000 both families.

When both families book together, one team follows the whole story, from kanyadan and sindoor pote to bidaai. That costs less than two separate bookings, and it keeps the photos consistent.

## Do wedding photographers charge for travel outside Kathmandu?

We work in zones:

- **Zone A, Kathmandu Valley** (Kathmandu, Lalitpur, Bhaktapur, Kirtipur): travel is included.
- **Zone A+, Valley edge** (Banepa, Dhulikhel, Panauti, Nagarkot, Chitlang): transport is NPR 4,000 per day.
- **Zone B, up to about six hours by road** (Pokhara, Chitwan, Gorkha, Hetauda, Nuwakot): coverage is plus 15%, and the family covers transport, rooms and meals for the team.
- **Zone C, far or by flight** (Butwal, Biratnagar, Janakpur, Birgunj, Dharan, Nepalgunj, Dhangadhi and the hill districts): coverage is plus 25%, with the same travel terms as zone B, plus flights where the road takes eight hours or more.
- **Outside Nepal** (India, Bhutan, Thailand, Dubai and beyond): custom quote.

## How to budget for wedding photography and film

A few things we tell every family:

- **List your events first.** Write down every ceremony and roughly how long it runs. Then compare single events against a package.
- **Decide on film early.** A highlight film or reels change the team you need, so it is easier to plan from the start.
- **Book both families together if you can.** It usually saves money and gives you one consistent set of photos.
- **Keep a small buffer.** An extra hour or an extra photographer on the busiest day is common.
- **Ask what is in writing.** We give delivery dates in writing, and a sneak peek within 48 hours.

## When should you book a wedding photographer in Nepal?

Popular wedding dates fill quickly, especially in the main season. Booking 90 days or more ahead gets you our 5% early bird discount, and it gives us time to plan your pre-wedding shoot and learn your family's rituals. If your date is closer than that, still ask.
