Rituals run on their own time, and that's part of the beauty. A few buffers keep the day relaxed.

## Our rules of thumb
- Add 30 minutes to the janti arrival time.
- Plan family group photos straight after the main ritual, while everyone is dressed and present.
- Keep 20 minutes for couple portraits in the best light of the day.
- Share the timeline with us a week before, and we'll suggest where to add breathing room.
